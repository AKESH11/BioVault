## Full Description (4000 chars max)

BioVault is the first media authentication protocol that cryptographically proves your photos and videos are genuine — captured by a real person, on a real device, at a verified time.

HOW IT WORKS
Every capture is authenticated with three layers of proof:

1. BIOMETRIC LIVENESS — Your heart rate is silently extracted from facial video using remote photoplethysmography (rPPG). This proves a living person was present during capture — no deepfake or AI can replicate this.

2. DEVICE FINGERPRINT — Your camera's unique sensor noise pattern (PRNU) cryptographically binds each capture to your specific device. Like a digital DNA for your camera.

3. BLOCKCHAIN ANCHOR — A tamper-proof hash of your media + biometric proof is permanently recorded on the Polygon blockchain. Anyone can verify it was never altered.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• All biometric processing happens ON YOUR DEVICE — raw signals never leave your phone
• Zero-knowledge proofs let you prove authenticity without revealing personal data
• Only cryptographic hashes (irreversible) are stored on-chain
• GDPR and BIPA compliant

DECENTRALIZED STORAGE
Media is stored on IPFS (InterPlanetary File System) — no single point of failure, no censorship, no corporate control over your content.

USE CASES
• Journalists: Prove footage is unedited and originated from the field
• Legal: Court-admissible proof of media authenticity
• Insurance: Verify claim photos are genuine and untampered
• Social Media: Combat deepfakes and misinformation
• Art/NFT: Prove original creation with biometric authorship

VERIFY ANY MEDIA
Anyone can verify a BioVault-authenticated media file — no account needed. Just scan or enter the verification hash.

AUTHENTICITY TOKENS
Mint ERC-721 NFTs that certify your media's authenticity, linked to the blockchain anchor and biometric proof.

Open source. Transparent. Verifiable.
